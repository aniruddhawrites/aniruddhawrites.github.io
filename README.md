# Aniruddha Writes

A personal blog by Aniruddha focused on Data Engineering, Analytics Architecture, Amazon Redshift, Business Intelligence, AI, and real-world enterprise technology lessons.

**Aniruddha Writes** is a collection of practical insights, case studies, architecture discussions, troubleshooting stories, and lessons learned from nearly two decades of working with enterprise data platforms and analytics solutions.

Topics covered include:

* Data Engineering
* Data Architecture
* Amazon Redshift
* Power BI

* Business Intelligence
* Performance Optimization
* AI & Automation
* Technology Leadership
* Career Growth in Data & Analytics
